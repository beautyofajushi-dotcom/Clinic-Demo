Dental Surgeon & Founder

Dr. Vipin Agrawal

BDS, MDS — Conservative Dentistry & Endodontics

Dr. Agrawal believes great dentistry is equal parts science, art and empathy. After graduating with a gold medal in Conservative Dentistry and specialising in microscopic endodontics, he returned to his home region with a goal — bring metro-grade dental care to Mathura.

Eighteen years and 12,000+ smiles later, he is known for two things: hands so gentle that anxious patients fall asleep mid-treatment, and honesty so blunt that he has occasionally talked patients out of treatment they didn't need.
