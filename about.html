Our Story

From one chair to twelve thousand smiles

Agrawal Dental Studio began in 2008 as a single-chair clinic near Vishram Ghat, founded on one belief — the people of Mathura deserve world-class dental care without travelling to Delhi for it.

Today the studio has grown into a full-floor practice with digital diagnostics, laser dentistry and a sterilization bay that rivals any metro hospital. Yet the promise remains untouched: every patient is still treated personally by Dr. Vipin Agrawal, every plan is explained in plain language, and every bill matches its estimate.
